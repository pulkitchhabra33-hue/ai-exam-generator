📚 About AI Exam Generator

Create exam papers with the help of AI. Spend less time preparing question papers and more time focusing on learning and teaching.

🎯 Our Purpose

AI Exam Generator is designed to simplify exam-paper creation for teachers, educators, and students. Our goal is to make it easier to prepare structured question papers based on your selected requirements.

⚙️ What You Can Do

  • Generate question papers for selected subjects and classes.
  • Choose difficulty, exam type, time limit, and total marks.
  • Organize questions into sections and question types.
  • Upload previous papers for additional reference.
  • Include answer keys and solutions where supported.
  • Download generated PDFs and revisit papers through My Papers.

✨ How It Works

  1. Enter your exam details and topics.
  2. Configure sections and question types.
  3. Generate your paper using AI.
  4. Review the result and download your PDF.

Always review generated questions and answers before using a paper in a formal examination.

👥 Who Is It For?

The platform is intended for teachers, tutors, educational institutions, and students who want a convenient way to prepare or practise with question papers.
